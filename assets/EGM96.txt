The binary grid contains NGA EGM96 geoid undulations in centimeters, encoded as
signed, big-endian 16-bit integers and compressed with gzip. The grid is 1440 by
721, starts at longitude -180 and latitude +90, and has 0.25-degree spacing.

Source: https://cdn.proj.org/us_nga_egm96_15.tif
